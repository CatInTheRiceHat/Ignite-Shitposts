====================================================================
SPECS
====================================================================
Format:      VERTICAL 9:16 (1080 x 1920). Say so if this one should be
             landscape like "All Due This Week"; the layout changes.
Length:      About 45 seconds (91 words, about 33 seconds of speech)
Narrator:    Arthur (only voice). Sol is silent and answers by acting.
Course:      See "Check before generating": the name and the last line
             depend on which course this ad is for.
Ending:      No question at the end. Sol looks up and listens through
             the last line, nods, and only then makes the happy face.
Captions:    Must match the narration below word for word. Any take that
             differs gets regenerated, not cut around.
Pacing:      Narration is never sped up. If a take runs long, extend the
             clip.


====================================================================
THE STORY IN ONE LINE
====================================================================
The group project is a sandcastle, due before the tide comes in. Sol is
building all of it while his group, three other Sols, lies around on the
beach. Arthur gives him five tips, and each one pulls the group in, until
all four Sols finish the castle together.

The beach and the castle are the course card's own art (Sol on a
sandcastle at sunset), so the last shot turns into the end card.


====================================================================
THE FOUR SOLS (lock this first)
====================================================================
All four are the same approved 3D Sol. One simple one-piece thing tells
each one apart, and it never comes off:

  OUR SOL      nothing extra. The only one working.
  SLEEPY SOL   asleep on a striped towel under a beach umbrella (Zzz).
  RING SOL     wears a pink swim ring round his middle.
  HAT SOL      a small straw hat on his flame tip, staring at the sea.

The names above are the names written on the signs. Lettering is never
painted: every sign is drawn once and laid over the painted clip.


====================================================================
THE SET (use in every clip)
====================================================================
- A wide beach at sunset: peach sky, pale sand, slow waves on the left.
- The castle site in the middle: one finished tower, a half-built wall,
  a bucket and a shovel. It grows from clip to clip and is finished in
  Clip 8, matching the card art (three towers, a flag, shells, the red
  bucket and shovel).
- A small sign staked in the sand beside it: "GROUP PROJECT".
- The three other Sols are off to the right, well away from the castle.
- Captions sit in the ad band (70% of the way down). Keep faces and
  signs above it.


====================================================================
CHARACTER NOTES
====================================================================
- Sol: no mouth, no eyebrows. Emotion comes from the eyes, the marks,
  the body (hops, leans, nods) and a bounce on every change of face.
- Faces are from the 38-expression set, by exact name. This script
  uses: normal, determined, panic, sigh, angry, idea, smug, startled,
  confused, asleep, scared, singing, surprised, happy, joy, sparkle.
- One cartoon gag per clip, never the same one twice:
    1 the frantic zip between jobs      5 the flinch, then smug
    2 the deadpan look into the camera  6 heads popping into frame
    3 the light bulb                    7 the innocent whistling
    4 the double take                   8 the group cheer
- Annoyed and tired belong to Clips 1 and 2 only. From Tip 1 on he is
  calm and busy.
- Nobody reaches for anything with an arm. Actions are hop, bump, lean,
  nub up, nod. Props are one piece each.
- Emotion sounds on the big beats only, not on every face.

====================================================================
FULL NARRATION / CAPTION TEXT (word for word)
====================================================================
Sol, is that your group project? Wait, are you building the whole thing
by yourself?

And that's your group? Okay, here are five things that'll fix this.

First, split the project into small pieces.

Second, put one name on each piece.

Third, set a deadline before the real deadline.

Fourth, keep everything in one doc the whole team can see.

And fifth, ask one specific person, instead of saying, can someone.

Ignite's free Social Impact and Project Development course teaches you
how to take an idea and actually make it happen.

Word count: 91 (about 33 seconds of speech at a natural pace)


====================================================================
WHERE EACH TIP COMES FROM
====================================================================
The Summer 2026 live course "Project and Nonprofit Building", Day 2
("Planning & Execution"), slide "Build your game plan":
Tip 1  Small pieces      -> "Break the big goal into smaller tasks",
                            "1 List Tasks"
Tip 2  One name each     -> "2 Assign Roles: who's doing each one?"
Tip 3  Early deadline    -> "3 Set Deadlines: one deadline per task"
                            (the "before the real deadline" part is ours)
Tip 4  One doc           -> "4 Team Document" (the Team Workspace doc
                            with its Task / Team Member / Due table)
Tip 5  Ask one person    -> not in the slides; it is ours
