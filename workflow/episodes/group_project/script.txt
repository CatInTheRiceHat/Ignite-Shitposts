SPECS
====================================================================
Format:      SQUARE 1:1 (1080 x 1080). Built square, not cropped.
Length:      About 45 seconds (91 words, about 33 seconds of speech)
Narrator:    Arthur (only voice). Every Sol is silent and acts.
Course:      See "Check before generating": the name and the last line
             depend on which course this ad is for.
Ending:      No question at the end. Sol looks up and listens through
             the last line, nods, and only then makes the happy face.
Captions:    Must match the narration below word for word. Any take that
             differs gets regenerated, not cut around.
Pacing:      Narration is never sped up. If a take runs long, extend the
             clip.


====================================================================
THE STORY IN ONE LINE
====================================================================
It is the Sandcastle Challenge: teams of Sols on a beach, judging at
six. The team next door builds together like a machine. Our Sol is
building his team's whole castle alone while his three teammates lie
around. Arthur gives him five tips, each one pulls a teammate in, and
the four of them finish the castle and take the blue ribbon.

The beach and the finished castle are the course card's own art (Sol on
a sandcastle at sunset), so the last shot turns into the end card.


====================================================================
THE SQUARE LAYOUT (use in every clip)
====================================================================
- The action sits in the middle and upper two thirds of the square.
- Captions: lower third, centred. Nothing important in the bottom 10%.
- Keep faces and signs above the caption band.
- End card (Clip 8): on the right 60% of the frame, stopping above the
  captions. Sol and the castle stay on the left.


====================================================================
THE SOLS (lock this first)
====================================================================
Every Sol is the same approved 3D model, 1 to 1. One simple one-piece
thing tells each teammate apart, and it never comes off:

  OUR SOL      nothing extra. The only one working.
  SLEEPY SOL   asleep on a striped towel under a beach umbrella (Zzz).
  RING SOL     wears a pink swim ring round his middle.
  HAT SOL      a small straw hat on his flame tip, staring at the sea.

  THE OTHER TEAM   three plain Sols in the background, one plot over,
                   passing buckets in a line. Their castle is already
                   tall and has a BLUE flag. They are never close up.

The names above are the names written on the signs. Lettering is never
painted: every sign is drawn once and laid over the painted clip.


====================================================================
THE SET (use in every clip)
====================================================================
- A wide beach at sunset: peach sky, pale sand, slow waves.
- A banner on two poles over the sand: "SANDCASTLE CHALLENGE".
- A contest clock on a post beside it, and a board under it:
  "JUDGING 6:00".
- Two plots marked out with rope. The far one: the other team and their
  tall castle. The near one: ours, with one finished tower, a half-built
  wall, a red bucket and a shovel. Ours grows from clip to clip and is
  finished in Clip 8, matching the card art (three towers, a flag,
  shells, the red bucket and shovel).
- Our three teammates are off to the right of our plot, well away from
  the castle.


====================================================================
CHARACTER NOTES
====================================================================
- Sol: no mouth, no eyebrows. Emotion comes from the eyes, the marks,
  the body (hops, leans, nods) and a bounce on every change of face.
- Faces are from the 38-expression set, by exact name. This script
  uses: normal, determined, panic, sigh, angry, thinking, idea, smug,
  startled, confused, asleep, scared, singing, surprised, happy, joy,
  sparkle, content.
- One cartoon gag per clip, never the same one twice:
    1 the frantic zip between jobs      5 the flinch, then smug
    2 the deadpan look into the camera  6 heads popping into frame
    3 the light bulb                    7 the innocent whistling
    4 the double take                   8 the group cheer
- Annoyed and tired belong to Clips 1 and 2 only. From Tip 1 on he is
  calm and busy.
- Nobody reaches for anything with an arm. Actions are hop, bump, lean,
  nub up, nod. Props are one piece each.
- Emotion sounds on the big beats only, not on every face.

FULL NARRATION / CAPTION TEXT (word for word)
====================================================================
Sol, how's the sandcastle challenge going? Wait, are you building the
whole thing by yourself?

And that's your team? Okay, here are five things that'll fix this.

First, split the project into small pieces.

Second, put one name on each piece.

Third, set a deadline before the real deadline.

Fourth, keep everything in one doc the whole team can see.

And fifth, ask one specific person, instead of saying, can someone.

Ignite's free Social Impact and Project Development course teaches you
how to take an idea and actually make it happen.

Word count: 91 (about 33 seconds of speech at a natural pace)
