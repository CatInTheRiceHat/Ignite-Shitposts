TITLE
Why Does a Flame Always Point Up?

CAPTION
Tilt a candle and the flame still points up. Why? Sol and the Narrator find out: hot air rises, cooler air slides in underneath, and that rising stream stretches the flame into a teardrop. In orbit there is no "up", so the flame turns into a little blue ball. 🔥
