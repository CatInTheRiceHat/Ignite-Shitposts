DECISIONS MADE WHERE THE SCRIPT LEFT A CHOICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goggles: NONE, in every clip. The script allows all clips or none. His eyes carry every expression in this ad
  (rings, chevrons, sparkles, worry lines), and goggles would sit on top of them. The course card art shows him in
  goggles; that is the only place.
Length: 46.3 s, not 40. Arthur's takes hold 40.6 s of speech once dead air is trimmed, and the script says the voice
  is never sped up ("extend the clip"). Nothing is sped up or stretched.
Clip 8 is two shots (08 and 09). Its narration is 10.6 s plus the nod, and a paint clip can be at most 10 s. Shot 08
  is the wide on the finished poster; shot 09 cuts closer for "So, is it ready now?" and the nod.
Clip 4 is a high angle, not straight top-down: from straight above his face cannot be seen, and the script gives
  him two expressions in that clip.
Sign-up link on the card: ignitefindyourfire.org/program (the site's courses page; /apply redirects there).
Poster locked before any paint: header board "Does salt make ice melt faster?"; left wing: peach card
  "Changed: amount of salt", blue card "Measured: minutes to melt", three green tags Trial 1/2/3; middle: bar graph,
  plain ice tall and blue, salted ice shorter and yellow, y axis "minutes to melt"; right wing: the yellow notebook
  page "What went wrong".
Still Elaine's to confirm before the ad runs (script, "check before generating"): that the course is open to middle
  schoolers (the site's Moodle catalog preview tags it "College & professional") and that it is free (the site's FAQ
  says the courses are planned as free). The narration and the card both say "free".
Music: the cut uses a song from assets/music/ as usual. Those are game soundtracks; a paid ad normally needs music
  she has the rights to. A cut with no music (voice and sound effects only) is built beside it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT IS MADE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Voice: Arthur (config/voices.json), ten takes in audio/raw/, dead air trimmed into audio/edit/ by audio/trim_takes.py
  (pauses over 0.3 s shortened; nothing sped up). Every take was transcribed and matches its line.
Skeleton: skeleton/rig.py + clips.py, rendered headless by render_clip.py. Sol is the approved SEPARATE-EYES model
  (assets/sol-3d/eyes/sol-eyes.blend): every expression, blink and glance in the script is keyed, with the bounce
  between emotions (eyes_rig.emotion_at). The rooms are blocked in simple real colours, one palette for the house.
Lettering: NEVER painted. make_art.py draws each piece once (overlays/art/); the same file is used where Sol makes
  the piece and on the finished poster. The pieces sit on OVL_ planes in the Blender scenes, are rendered alone in
  each shot's perspective (render_clip.py MODE=ovl) and laid over the painted clip by overlays/composite.py, which
  also adds the pop-up labels (clips 4, 5), the course card (clips 8, 9), a slow push on the locked-off shots, and
  keeps lettering behind Sol with his silhouette from the skeleton (MODE=mask). Every camera with lettering is
  locked off. Fonts: Patrick Hand (OFL, overlays/fonts/) for the handwriting (Gochi Hand was dropped: its "lt" read as "H"); Rammetto One and Poppins on the card.
Paint: Gemini Omni Flash 1.2, mode edit, 720p. Image references: a style frame, Sol's turnaround sheet and the
  expression sheet. Clip 01 was painted first (style reference: the coffee episode's kitchen frame, treatment only);
  its frame at 1.0 s is refs/style-frame-clip01.png, the style reference for every other clip. Prompts:
  prompts/build.py writes prompts/0N-paint.txt.
Edit: make_edit.py writes edit.json (captions are the script's words, timed from Whisper's word times), built with
  scripts/episode_edit.py. Captions sit in a band centred at 70% of the height and at most 840 px wide, out of the
  bottom fifth and off the right edge (script, framing rule). Sound effects are the candle set.

SHOTS (times on each clip's own clock; voice start in brackets)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
01 hook, 6.3 s. Bedroom desk, blank trifold, wall clock; locked-off medium wide. "Sol, what are you doing?" [0.25]
   "Wait, your science project's due in five minutes?" [2.5]. Looks at the poster, up at the clock (1.7), Shocked +
   hop + indigo snap (2.55), Worried (4.7), nod (5.5).
02 setup, 3.0 s. Slow push in. "Okay, here are five things that'll save it." [0.2]. Worried, full indigo; leans in;
   > < squeezed shut (1.95).
03 tip 1, 5.4 s. Hallway whiteboard, slight low angle. "First, write your question at the top so judges know what
   you were testing." [0.2]. Hop-tap (1.28-1.76), the question is written on from 1.52; blink (3.25); Normal and the
   indigo fading (3.7); second hop-tap (4.15).
04 tip 2, 4.0 s. Kitchen counter from high above. "Second, say the one thing you changed and what you measured."
   [0.12]. Bumps the salt shaker with his side (0.95-1.5), label "Changed" pops (1.9); turns and hops toward the
   timer (2.45), label "Measured" pops (3.3). About half indigo.
05 tip 3, 4.85 s. Freezer on the counter, eye level. "Third, say how many times you ran it, because one try could
   just be luck." [0.2]. Nudges the door (0.45), it swings open (0.75-1.7), fog; O O surprised (1.45); tags
   Trial 1, 2, 3 pop (1.7, 2.0, 2.3); Normal (3.3).
06 tip 4, 5.15 s. Wall of sticky notes, wide. "Fourth, turn your numbers into a graph, since nobody reads a wall
   of numbers." [0.2]. Pushes, > < (0-1.1); the notes fall from 1.1 and the graph is underneath; Surprised
   mid-bounce (2.0); graph labels fade in (3.0); Normal (3.9).
07 tip 5, 5.0 s. Kitchen counter, close from above. "And fifth, write down what went wrong, because real scientists
   always do." [0.2]. Looks down at the puddle; Worried with a quick indigo flicker (1.0); one hop beside the
   notebook (1.66), the heading appears (1.95); blink (2.72); Normal, looking up (2.9).
08 payoff, a move across the poster, 9.3 s (from cut v6; the locked-off wide it replaced is in the take list).
   "Ignite's free Scientific Research and Data Analysis course teaches you how real research works, from collecting
   data to analyzing it." [0.25]. The camera opens on the whole finished poster (the question readable), glides in
   and travels across it: the "What went wrong" page (2.0), the graph (4.1), the Changed and Measured cards and the
   three trials (6.0), then swings left to Sol (7.5), sparkle eyes, two bounces with his feet planted, a blink (8.1).
   The course card pops in at 1.6. He stands clear of every piece of lettering. Lettering is a per-frame pass
   (MODE=ovlseq). In the edit a push-in on him (5.9-7.1 s, 1.3x) lifts his face clear of the captions.
09 payoff, the nod, 3.3 s. "So, is it ready now?" [0.25]. Looking up; Sparkles and two nods (1.95-2.9); holds to the
   last frame. No black.
