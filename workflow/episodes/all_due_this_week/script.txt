DECISIONS MADE WHERE THE SCRIPT LEFT A CHOICE, AND WHERE THE AD DIFFERS FROM IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Length: 44.1 s, not 40. Arthur's ten takes hold 37.4 s of speech once dead air is trimmed, and the script says the
  voice is never sped up ("extend the clip"). Nothing is sped up or stretched.
The pile (BEFORE): a thick stack of worksheets with a yellow sticky note "Math homework"; the piano music standing
  behind a soccer ball on top of it; the group-project poster board (white, blue sticky note "Group project") leaning on
  the wall; the calendar page (red band, "TEST FRIDAY" ringed in red) leaning in front of the poster.
The desk (AFTER): the homework in one short neat stack with a green check mark over its note; a pencil cup with a pink
  "Plan B" sticky note (a note lying flat on the desk cannot be read from eye level); the calendar page small, pinned to
  the wall with a red pin. The end card covers all of this from 1.4 s into the clip.
End card: built to her end-card prompt, with the last ad's fonts and orange (overlays/make_end_card.py):
  - site orange #F07B2A, rounded; the course's own card art UNCROPPED on top (Ignite Website/public/courses/
    Resilience & Stress Management.jpg), rounded corners; Poppins for the course name and the small line, Rammetto One
    for "Join Ignite today!".
  - The course name is written "Resilience & Stress-Management", WITH the hyphen, because her prompt says "exactly as
    written on the course card" and both the website's card and Emma's outline write it that way. Her script wrote it
    without the hyphen. One word in make_end_card.py changes it.
  - The card is on the right two thirds but stops at y = 824 of 1080, not at the bottom: the narrator is still talking
    while it is up, and the captions (which must be on screen word for word) need the strip under it.
  - art/end_card.png says "Link in description" (YouTube); art/end_card_web.png says
    forms.ignitefindyourfire.org/t/oqB8WMKbJ6us (website, presentations). A cut exists with each.
  - It slides in from the right over half a second, 1.0 s into clip 8, and holds to the last frame.
Flame colour: the script is followed (part purple, full purple at the shock, still purple in tip 1, draining during the
  breaths, a flicker at tip 5). This is not what her notes on the science ad asked for ("during the tip sequences sol
  ... should be calm"): here the purple in tip 1 is what the breathing clip drains, so it was kept. If she wants the
  tips calm in this ad too, clips 3 and 7 change (clip 3's purple is laid on in the edit and comes off for free).
Clip 3: each thing turns half round in the air and lands with its blank back to the camera, so no lettering has to
  ride a spinning prop. The boxes are low, so every thing stays in sight in its box.
Clip 5: "lights up" and "dim" are done in the edit (a warm glow around the homework, the other two darkened) from
  masks of the skeleton, not by the painter. The poster and the music lean on small wooden props.
Clip 6: Sol hop-taps the board's lower left corner (his arm cannot reach the lettering); "Plan B" is written on under
  Plan A. A door and a potted plant fill the right of the hallway.
Clip 7: he nudges the sketch with his side (no arm reach) and it slides away across the desk.
Clip 8: one paint clip, 10.0 s, the same camera as clip 1.
Captions: Rammetto One 72 px, white with a 9 px orange outline, centred at y = 895: the lower third, above the bottom
  10% of the frame. Up to 34 characters, because the frame is wide.
Music: "No More What Ifs" (the instrumental version in assets/music/), Elaine's pick, from cut v4. Before it: v1 "A
  Little Lesson" (the coffee episode's song), v2 and v3 "Days of Sisters" (my pick). Those are game soundtracks; a paid ad normally needs music she has the rights to,
  so a cut with no music (voice and sound effects only) is built beside it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT IS MADE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Voice: Arthur (config/voices.json), ten takes in audio/raw/, dead air trimmed into audio/edit/ by audio/trim_takes.py.
  Every take was transcribed. Take 06 first came back with a sentence of gibberish in front of the line
  (audio/rejected/06-take1-extra-words.wav) and was regenerated. The cut's audio is the script word for word.
Skeleton: skeleton/rig.py + clips.py, rendered headless by render_clip.py, 1280 x 720 at 24 fps. Sol is the APPROVED
  separate-eyes model (assets/sol-3d/eyes/approved-2026-11-26/): its six expressions are the script's list. Every
  expression, blink and glance is keyed, with the bounce between emotions. Sol stands on the left third in every
  clip, his feet about 70% of the way down the frame, so the captions have the surface under him.
Lettering: NEVER painted. overlays/make_art.py draws each piece once (overlays/art/, ink only); the same file is used
  every time the piece appears. The pieces sit on OVL_ planes in the Blender scenes and are rendered as a pass for
  EVERY frame with everything in front of them cut out (render_clip.py MODE=ovlseq, a holdout pass: new in this ad),
  so lettering follows a moving camera (clip 2) or a moving prop (clips 3, 5, 7) and hides behind whatever passes in
  front. The whiteboard uses single planes (MODE=ovl) so "Plan B" can be wiped on. overlays/composite.py lays it all
  over the painted clips and adds the glow, the tint, the slow push on the locked-off shots and the end card.
Paint: Gemini Omni Flash 1.2, mode edit, 720p, 16:9. Image references: a style frame, Sol's turnaround sheet and the
  approved expression sheet. Clip 1 was painted first with the science ad's style frame (the same house); its frame at
  0.125 s is refs/style-frame-clip01.png, the style frame for the rest. Clip 4 also got the course's card art.
  Prompts: prompts/build.py writes prompts/0N-paint.txt; media ids are in prompts/media.json.
Edit: make_edit.py writes edit.json (captions are the script's words, timed from Whisper's word times), built with
  scripts/episode_edit.py, which gained optional width / height / caption_size / caption_stroke for landscape.
  Sound effects are the candle set.
