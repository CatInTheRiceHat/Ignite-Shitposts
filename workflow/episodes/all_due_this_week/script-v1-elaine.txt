SPECS
====================================================================
Format:      LANDSCAPE 16:9 (1920 x 1080). Built for landscape, not
             cropped from vertical.
Where it runs: YouTube, the Ignite website, school presentations,
             Google in-stream ads.
Length:      About 40 seconds
Narrator:    Arthur (only voice). Sol is silent and answers by acting.
Course:      Resilience & Stress Management (developer: Emma Zhou)
Ending:      Sol nods on the last clip and holds.
Captions:    Must match the narration below word for word. Any take that

====================================================================
THE STORY IN ONE LINE
====================================================================
Sol has way too much due this week. Arthur gives him five tips from the
course, and each one clears part of the pile, until the week looks
doable.

Opening question: Is all of this due this week? (Sol nods, purple)
Ending question:  Does this week feel doable now? (Sol nods, orange)


====================================================================
THE LANDSCAPE LAYOUT (use in every clip)
====================================================================
- SOL ON THE LEFT THIRD. THE STRESS ON THE RIGHT TWO THIRDS.
- The right side is where each tip does its work: sorting, shrinking,
  lighting up, getting replaced.
- Across the video the right side goes from cluttered to calm. The
  final frame should feel almost empty and balanced.
- Captions: lower third, centered. Nothing important in the bottom
  10% of the frame.

Note: this layout does NOT crop well to 9:16. If you also want a
vertical version, stack it instead (Sol on the bottom, stress on top)
and regenerate the clips.

====================================================================
FULL NARRATION / CAPTION TEXT (word for word)
====================================================================
Sol, what's all that? Wait, is all of this due this week?

Okay, here are five things that'll help.

First, sort it into what you control, what you can influence, and what
you have to accept.

Second, take a few slow breaths so your body can calm down.

Third, pick the one thing that matters most and start there.

Fourth, have a backup plan, so one thing going wrong doesn't wreck
everything.

And fifth, let your first try be messy. Rough drafts are supposed to
be rough.

Ignite's free Resilience and Stress Management course teaches all of
this and more. So, does this week feel doable now?

Word count: 107 (about 36 seconds of speech at a natural pace)
