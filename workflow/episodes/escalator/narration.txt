An electric motor turns toothed wheels that pull a chain along each side of the steps.

Each step has front and back wheels following separate tracks. The tracks guide those wheels at different heights, keeping the surface you stand on horizontal.

Near the top, the tracks bring neighboring steps to the same height, forming a flat surface before the steps turn underneath.
