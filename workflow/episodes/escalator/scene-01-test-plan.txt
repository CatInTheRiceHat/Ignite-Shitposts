SCENE 01 — USER FRAME APPROVAL FIRST

1. Generate the first and last stills using the ORIGINAL lantern character.
2. Show BOTH to the user, labeled first/last and version. Include the original
   reference for comparison. WAIT for their explicit approval of BOTH.
3. Record their actual approval and pass the frame gate. Only then prepare
   the Omni Flash 1.2 request (gemini_omni_flash_1_2, image-to-video, 1080p,
   9:16, 5 seconds, approved start_image AND end_image).
4. A dedicated reviewer inspects actual motion against those approved stills
   and the lantern reference: face/eyes, painted flame and wispy tip, compact
   limbs, both rails, foot contacts, direction, unique marker and ending hold.
5. Failed or uncertain review blocks production. Preserve all takes. Do not
   submit another video or continue the other scenes without the right approvals.
