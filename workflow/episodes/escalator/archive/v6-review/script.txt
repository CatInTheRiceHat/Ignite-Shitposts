SETTING AND CONTINUITY
One upward-running mall escalator, lower floor on screen left and upper landing on screen right. The rising steps always travel left to right; the hidden return run travels right to left. Warm painted mall lighting. One step has an orange editorial highlight so the viewer can track it. Sol rides that step at the opening, then stays on the upper landing throughout the explanation. Close-ups of other steps do not inherit the orange highlight.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Simplified rounded mall architecture in soft cream and muted teal, broad painted shadows, warm orange accents. Reduce background contrast around Sol's face and each mechanism detail. Keep the texture visibly painted.
Use exaggerated anticipation, squash/stretch, fast eye changes, whole-body takes, flame-tip follow-through, and short held poses. Expressions carry the performance without a mouth.
Use oversized, simplified diagram details and cartoon attention effects. The drive chain remains connected, the step bodies remain rigid, and supporting wheels stay on their assigned guide paths.

SOL AND THE NARRATOR
When the Narrator addresses visible Sol, Sol looks up toward a fixed point just above the camera, acknowledging someone outside the scene. His eyes lead, then the flame body and tip follow. He can look at a mechanism on a directed cue, then return to the voice. Keep both eyes readable and leave room for the upward body tilt.

EYE EXPRESSION KEY
Neutral: The two original navy vertical ovals at their original home positions on the cream face; no separate pupils.
Surprise: Both ovals become large round navy shapes, roughly one and a half times their neutral width, still separated and contained within the cream face.
Questioning: The eye nearer the gaze target is a tall oval; the other eye is shorter and flatter. Body lean establishes the gaze direction.
Focus: Both eyes compress into short horizontal navy shapes for a held scrutinizing look.
Tracking: The original ovals lean and compress toward the moving object's side, staying near their home positions; body rotation follows the eye change.
Delight: Two thick upward-arched navy crescents, like closed smiling eyes. These are the existing eyes changing shape, not added eyebrows.
Listening up: Two original-colored navy ovals, slightly taller and angled upward on the cream face, paired with the face and whole flame body tipping up toward the Narrator just above the camera. No separate pupils or added facial features.
