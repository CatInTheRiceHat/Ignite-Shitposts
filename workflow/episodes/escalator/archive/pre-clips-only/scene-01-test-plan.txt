These are action targets; do not accelerate the eventual dialogue to fit them.

Pass criteria
- Action is visible from the first second; no greeting, static opening hold,
  introductory camera move or delayed disappearance.
- Both feet, S17 and the stationary plate are simultaneously readable during
  transfer. At least one foot has believable support until both reach the floor.
- No floating foot, foot skating, sinking through metal or switch to a different
  tread. Contact shadows and handrail contact agree with the painted space.
- Sol's compact layered flame, tiny limbs, mouthless face and original navy
  eyes remain recognizable. One simple gaze/lean reaction reads at phone size.
- Sol occupies about 48% of picture height and the key tread is at least 54px
  wide in a 360 × 640 review. Plate edge and feet are unobscured.
- First and last frames share the same camera and structure; the end pose
  matches Scene 02's beginning. The video has one continuous locked camera.
- Arrows, captions, highlights, halos and sound effects are separate edit
  layers. S17's orange highlight follows the same object transform.
- If generated motion loses tread identity, rigid shape or contact, repair
  with controlled Remotion animation; do not hide the error with a crop.
