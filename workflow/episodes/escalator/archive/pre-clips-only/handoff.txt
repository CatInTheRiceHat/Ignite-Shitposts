Revised episode
- Six scenes, exactly 12 standalone still-image prompts and six continuous
  video prompts. Each scene retains one first/last pair and one motion brief.
- Script is 99 spoken words. The 43-second timeline is explicitly provisional.
  Separate voice files and the unconfirmed caption/voice cue plan agree.
- Scene 01 starts its grounded transfer immediately and exposes the exact
  original tread's disappearance under the fixed plate within the first second.
- Scene 02 uses one upward acknowledgment of Arthur, then follows that tread
  into the return run. The last pose is tracking downward, not a second look up.
- Scene 03 keeps one connected motor/drive/shaft/sprocket/chain system in view.
- Scene 04 depicts two near-side rollers in profile and four total per step.
- Scene 05 aligns neighboring treads before the leading rigid step goes under
  the exit plate and begins its return, using the same roller/track geometry.
- Scene 06 prioritizes tread return, the upward Exactly acknowledgment and a
  happy hold. A bounce is optional. Scene 03/06 pushes are Remotion-only.

Shared schematic and object route
references/mechanism-geometry.json defines one rigid step assembly, the
front/chain and rear/trailer roller offsets, two side chains sharing one clock,
and the S17 route ledger. tools/build-schematic.py derives five matching views:
full loop, drive, near-side roller profile with a far-pair depth inset, upper
exit and the projected return view. Colored lines and the orange reference
tread are editable annotations; generation prompts request clean neutral art.

S17 uses a single constant arc-speed phase through the provisional timeline,
including off-screen Scenes 03–05. The reference demonstrates the whole tread
under the plate at 0.8s, not merely its front axle crossing the plate edge.
The turn/track shapes are illustrative kinematics, not dimensional OEM CAD;
they do not certify collision clearances, manufacturing or maintenance use.
Use controlled Remotion motion if generated contacts or rigid geometry drift.

Primary mechanism provenance
The existing Transport for NSW explainer and Otis track catalog remain cited
in the plan. This revision also checked the primary US2039994A description:
https://patents.google.com/patent/US2039994A/en
It describes four rollers per step carriage, two driving chains and rollers
running on rails. The local schematic is an original simplified construction,
not a traced manufacturer drawing. The two older PDF endpoints were not
available to fetch during this pass; their prior project citations are retained.
