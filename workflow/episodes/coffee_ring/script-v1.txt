------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIALOGUE AND WHAT SOL DOES (read this first)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
01  Narrator: "Sol, why are you staring at the table?"
      Sol is crouched beside a dried coffee ring on a white saucer. He taps the dark
      rim with one arm, then the empty middle, then looks up.
    Narrator: "Ohh, you want to know why coffee dries into a ring, not a blob."
      Sol nods.
02  Narrator: "Watch. A fresh drop dries fastest at the edge."
      Sol sets a fresh drop on the saucer with a dropper. The camera dives in to
      table level. The drop's rim thins and shimmers as water leaves there first.
    Narrator: "But the edge is stuck to the saucer, so it can't shrink inward."
03  Narrator: "So water rushes out from the middle to refill it."
      Side cutaway of the drop: water flows outward along the bottom, then up the rim.
      Tiny brown coffee specks ride along. Sol watches and his gaze follows the flow.
04  Narrator: "And it carries every coffee speck with it, and dumps them at the edge."
      Overhead: the specks stream outward and pile up in a dark line at the rim while
      the middle clears and goes pale. Sol leans over and pushes his own little
      pile of specks along with his arm, then looks up.
05  Narrator: "So the ring isn't a mess. It's every speck, delivered to the edge. Got it?"
      Sol points at the finished ring, then nods twice.

HOW IT IS FILMED

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 01 — the ring — about 6.5s
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teaching purpose: the puzzle. The middle is empty and the edge is dark.
Set: cozy table, white saucer with a dried coffee ring, a coffee cup, a dropper bottle.
Camera: starts overhead and arcs down to a medium shot of Sol and the saucer.
Action: Sol taps the rim, taps the pale middle, looks up at the Narrator, nods.
Narrator: "Sol, why are you staring at the table?" then "Ohh, you want to know why coffee
  dries into a ring, not a blob."

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 02 — edge dries first, and it is stuck — about 6s
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teaching purpose: evaporation is fastest at the edge, and the edge stays pinned.
Set: same table. A fresh drop on a clean saucer; Sol holds the dropper.
Camera: dives from medium to table level, ending on the drop's rim.
Action: Sol squeezes out one drop and steps back. The rim shimmers and thins; the edge
  visibly stays put while the top gets flatter.
Narrator: "Watch. A fresh drop dries fastest at the edge." "But the edge is stuck to the
  saucer, so it can't shrink inward."

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 03 — water rushes outward — about 6s
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teaching purpose: the flow that refills the edge.
Set: side cutaway of the drop (clear dome shape), brown specks inside.
Camera: slow slide along the cutaway, following the flow.
Action: arrows of water flow out along the base, up the rim; specks ride along. Sol, small
  in frame beside the cutaway, follows the flow with his eyes.
Narrator: "So water rushes out from the middle to refill it."

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 04 — specks pile up — about 7s
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teaching purpose: the specks are what makes the ring.
Set: overhead of the drop, drying.
Camera: slow overhead push in, then a small tilt back.
Action: specks stream outward and pile into a dark line; the centre goes pale. Sol leans
  over the edge of frame and pushes his own small pile of specks outward with an arm.
Narrator: "And it carries every coffee speck with it, and dumps them at the edge."
Note: the specks are coffee, not particles coming off Sol.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 05 — Sol gets it — about 5s
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Teaching purpose: answer the opening question with the opening scene.
Set: as clip 01, the finished ring.
Camera: starts close on the ring, pulls back and rises to show Sol.
Action: Sol points at the ring, looks up, nods twice. End on the second nod; no black.
Narrator: "So the ring isn't a mess. It's every speck, delivered to the edge. Got it?"

Accuracy: the "coffee ring effect" (Deegan et al., Nature 1997): evaporation is faster at the
  pinned contact line, which drives an outward flow that carries suspended particles to the edge.
