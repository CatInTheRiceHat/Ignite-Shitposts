RESEARCH REFERENCES
Fire 400 ka — Pathways to Ancient Britain
https://www.pabproject.org/research-projects/fire-400-ka/
The research team distinguishes likely use of wildfires and maintained embers from intentional ignition. Early dates are uncertain; no first discoverer is identified.

Davis et al. — Earliest evidence of making fire
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41372405/
The study reports evidence interpreted as deliberate fire-making at Barnham around 400,000 years ago. This evidence date is not a known first invention or discoverer.

Berna et al. — Fire evidence at Wonderwerk Cave
https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/109/20/E1215.full.pdf
Burned bone and plant ash document burning in an archaeological context around one million years ago. This does not identify a person or establish the first ignition method.
