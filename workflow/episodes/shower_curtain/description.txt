Why Does Your Shower Curtain Creep Toward You?

Turn on the shower and the curtain creeps in at you. Why? Sol and the Narrator find out: falling water drags air down with it, and that air loops around into a little sideways whirlwind. The middle of a whirlwind is low pressure, so it pushes on the curtain a little less than the room outside does. The room wins and shoves the curtain in. It's not sneaking up on you. It's getting pushed.
